# Wivity

> Wivity Inc. is a Web3 governance consultancy that helps organizations form blockchain consortia and infrastructure DAOs, design Web3 infrastructure, and optimize decentralized governance through its Wivity Governance framework. Partners referenced on its site include the SunSpec Alliance and OMA3. Wivity is a 500 Global portfolio company and does not publish a public API, developer portal, or client libraries.
